# Review
- Most parts fit well, none were too loose nor too tight.
- Not a fan of stickers. Sadly had to put some on the Hubble statuette. The chrome plating of the bricks *do not* mesh well with the sticker, as shown.
- Flexible tubing on the wing section were a bit finnicky to work with, definitely the most challenging part of the build.
- The **armature** inside the cargo bay is supposed to hold up the Hubble statuette, but is too weak to support its weight.
- I was missing a small 1x2 diagonal part, and was provided a 1x2 curved part instead. Not noticeable, but departs from the manual.
- There's a degree of articulation for *all control surfaces*, as well as minimal gimbal on the thrusters.
- Landing gear isn't detailed, but can be placed wherever you want as the baseplate is mostly flat and blank. Chose to place mine nearer to the nose tip (to be more accurate with the real shuttle).
- Way more difficult than [[20260929-jaki-long-march-5|the Long March 5 model]]. Since most sections are interdependent, and the shuttle baseplate lacks stability which only gets built upon on the later steps (leading to unintentional disassembly for e.g. wings).
