# Review
- Most parts fit well, none were too loose nor too tight.
- No egregious stickers! Most come pre-printed with the plastic and don't interfere with the quality.
- Very repetitive. The build process for the thrusters are done twice.
- Highly parallelizable. Can work on this with multiple people easily as there's not much dependence between rockets, and between sections within rockets.
- A bit wobbly but stable enough not to fall. As you can imagine, the model's very top-heavy.
